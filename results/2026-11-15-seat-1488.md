## Claim
Selection never built a dark-matter sense in pigeons, because a spatially uniform oscillation carries no map information. But it did build a low-frequency pressure detector that a scalar field would drive as a by-product: the gas-filled middle ear and paratympanic organ, read against bone. This is conjecture, and the effect falls far short of the bird's threshold.

## Mechanism
1. A scalar φ of mass 4×10⁻¹⁶–4×10⁻¹⁴ eV oscillates at 0.1–10 Hz, the pigeon's infrasound band. It couples to the electromagnetic term (d_e) and the electron mass (d_me).
2. The Bohr radius scales as 1/(α·mₑ), so every solid breathes with strain h ≈ δα/α + δmₑ/mₑ.
3. Bone of the middle-ear cavity changes volume by 3h. The enclosed gas is set by temperature and atmospheric pressure, not by atomic size, so it does not follow.
4. This gives a differential pressure δp ≈ 3h·p_atm across the tympanum and paratympanic organ.
5. Hair cells transduce it, and infrasound-tuned fibres of the auditory nerve carry it as a narrow tone superimposed on the acoustic map.

## Size of the effect
- **Field strength:** equivalence-principle and clock bounds allow roughly h ≲ 10⁻¹⁸ at these masses (order of magnitude, from memory).
- **Pressure:** δp ≈ 3×10⁻¹³ Pa.
- **Threshold:** the pigeon's is about 0.1–1 Pa near 1 Hz, and natural microbarom noise is 10⁻²–10⁻¹ Pa.
- **Shortfall:** about 11–12 orders of magnitude. A Sun-bound scalar halo, which has been proposed near 10⁻¹⁴ eV, could raise the amplitude 10²–10³ times, leaving about 9 orders.
- **Integration:** the line is narrow (Δf/f ≈ 10⁻⁶), but no known neural integrator holds phase for 10⁶ cycles.

## What we would observe
- **The signal:** a tone at one fixed frequency, phase-coherent worldwide with zero propagation delay. Real infrasound travels at 340 m/s.
- **Its modulation:** amplitude varies annually and with the sidereal day.
- **Behaviour:** globally synchronous orientation scatter or race losses, uncorrelated with local weather, geomagnetic indices or sonic booms.

## How to test it
- **Existing microbarometer data:** the international nuclear-test-monitoring infrasound network has about 60 arrays covering 0.01–10 Hz. Each microbarometer is the same sealed-cavity-versus-air device as the ear. Search two decades of archives for a zero-lag, array-coherent narrow line. My estimate of the reach is δp ≈ 10⁻⁸ Pa, or h ≈ 10⁻¹³.
- **Race records:** regress pigeon-race return speeds and loss rates across continents on the phase of any candidate line.
- **A built sensor:** an "artificial ear" made of a rigid silica cavity, gas and an optical membrane readout, run in a pressure-shielded vault.
- **Biology:** auditory-nerve recordings in pigeons sealed in a pressure chamber, looking for a residual narrow line.

## What would count against it
- **No line:** the network shows no zero-lag line down to 10⁻⁸ Pa, or atomic clocks push h below 10⁻²⁰ across 0.1–10 Hz.
- **No global synchrony:** race losses show no intercontinental synchrony once the planetary geomagnetic index (Kp) is removed.
- **Evolutionary check:** pigeon infrasound tuning is no sharper or deeper than in non-navigating relatives such as chickens and doves. That would confirm selection tracked acoustic maps only, and any dark-sector response is an unselected, sub-threshold leak.

- The transducer gain is only p_atm per unit strain. With microbarometer noise of about 10⁻³ Pa/√Hz at 1 Hz, strain noise is about 10⁻⁸/√Hz, against about 10⁻¹⁶/√Hz for optical cavities.
- Your archive reach of h ≈ 10⁻¹³ looks right by my estimate, but it sits five orders inside the region you say is already excluded.

- Thermodynamic fluctuation alone gives δT/T ≈ N^(-1/2), about 10⁻¹⁰ for 1 cm³ of gas. A middle ear of about 0.1 cm³ is worse.
- The avian pharyngotympanic tube and interaural canal vent the cavity, high-passing the signal at an unmeasured corner frequency.

**2. Ordinary explanations for a zero-lag global line.**
- **Timing pickup:** GPS pulse-per-second and digitizer artefacts at integer Hz, plus shared firmware.
- **Electromagnetic pickup:** Schumann resonance at 7.8 Hz and Pc1 geomagnetic pulsations (0.2–5 Hz) coupling into the displacement transducers. Both are near-zero-lag and global.
- **Microbaroms:** narrow peaks that drift with season.
- **Race data:** weekend scheduling, shared weather systems and Kp-correlated effects.

A real line is non-integer, about 10⁻⁶ wide and sidereally modulated. It is absent from a co-located magnetometer.

It costs weeks of one person's time. It settles the evolutionary question, though not the dark-sector one, which only the twin cell can address.
